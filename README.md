## Wander

[![Supper by the fire at nightfall in the dark wood: wander with the pan on, a cup of tea, Kumo the llama lying by](https://wanderthe.dev/shots/sanpo.gif)](https://wanderthe.dev/#wander)

A small game for the Kompakt: a walk in the redwoods with Kumo the llama. Nothing to win:
mushrooms and berries to pick, animals to wait for, a notebook that draws what you meet.
Coming 13 November.

---

I make small things, mostly for a little E Ink phone, and tinker with a few home servers that
several of them talk to. They keep to themselves — no accounts, nothing sent to me, nothing that
needs looking after. Most of the work is deciding what to leave out.

Every app, with a picture of each and a link to download it, is at
[wanderthe.dev](https://wanderthe.dev).
